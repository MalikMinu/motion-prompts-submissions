# MotionPrompts Submissions

This is the public submission inbox for **[MotionPrompts](https://motion-prompts.pages.dev)** — a gallery of AI motion prompts where every entry is actually tested before it is published.

## Submit a prompt

Use the **Submit a motion prompt** issue form in this repo. One prompt per issue.
Every submission is reviewed by a person, and the prompt is actually re-run by us
before anything goes live. If it passes, your entry is published with your name,
a link to your profile, and a link to your original post.

Before submitting, read the **[Contributor Terms](https://motion-prompts.pages.dev/terms)** —
the short version: the prompt and the video must be your own work, and you grant
MotionPrompts and its visitors a free-use licence (you keep your copyright).

No GitHub account? Use the form or the copy template on
**[motion-prompts.pages.dev/submit](https://motion-prompts.pages.dev/submit)** instead.

## Takedowns

Creator of an entry and want it removed, or believe an entry infringes your
rights? Use the **Takedown request** issue form. What we need and what happens
next is spelled out in the
[Contributor Terms, section 9](https://motion-prompts.pages.dev/terms#takedown).

## Why a separate repo?

The main MotionPrompts repo is private. This repo exists only to hold the two
public issue forms above — it has no code.
